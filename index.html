CBSE English Grammar Formula Sheet

Present Simple: Subject + V1(s/es) + Object. (e.g., She writes essays.)
Present Continuous: Subject + is/am/are + V1-ing. (e.g., They are preparing sets.)
Present Perfect: Subject + has/have + V3. (e.g., He has completed the chapter.)
Past Simple: Subject + V2 + Object. (e.g., The committee passed the resolution.)
Future Simple: Subject + will/shall + V1. (e.g., We shall conquer the boards.)
General Formula: Object + Auxiliary (be) + V3 (Past Participle) + by + Subject.
Assertive: "Chiranjibi Sir teaches grammar." → "Grammar is taught by Chiranjibi Sir."
Continuous: "Students are taking the test." → "The test is being taken by students."
Imperative: "Open the portal." → "Let the portal be opened."
Tense Shifts: Present Simple → Past Simple | Present Continuous → Past Continuous | Past Simple → Past Perfect.
Modals Shift: Will → Would | Can → Could | May → Might | Shall → Should.
Time Expressions: Today → that day | Tomorrow → the next day | Now → then | Here → there.
Must vs. Should: Must implies legal / strict moral obligation or high certainty; Should indicates advisory recommendation.
Few vs. A Few: Few denotes hardly any (negative sense); A few denotes some quantity (positive sense) for countable nouns.
Little vs. A Little: Little denotes almost none; A little denotes a small amount for uncountable nouns.
